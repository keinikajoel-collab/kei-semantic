About Kei Car Wash

Our story

Kei Car Wash was started to give drivers a dependable place to get their vehicles cleaned properly, without guesswork on price or quality. As part of Kei Group of Companies, we hold ourselves to the same standards of honesty and professionalism as the wider group.

What we stand for

  • Care: we treat your car as if it were our own.
  • Honesty: clear prices with no surprise charges.
  • Reliability: on time, every time, from booking to handover.

Part of Kei Group

Kei Car Wash is one of the businesses of Kei Group of Companies.
